Common questions

What will I learn in the AI MasterClass?

Five core topics: YouTube channel creation, motion graphics for brands, thumbnail creation, website building with AI and detailed prompt writing.

Who is the course for?

Students, young adults, creators and local business owners who want practical AI skills.

Do I need to know programming?

The five core topics focus on using AI tools. Programming is not one of them.
