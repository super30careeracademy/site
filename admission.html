Admission questions

Is there a free demo class?

Yes. New students can attend a free demo class before enrolling. Call or message us to book a slot.

What are the batch timings and sizes?

Morning 8 AM to 12 PM, afternoon 12 PM to 4 PM, evening 4 PM to 8 PM and weekend batches. Batches usually have 8 to 15 students.

Who can get a scholarship?

We offer merit based and need based support. Tick the scholarship box in the form and our counsellor will explain the options.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cash, UPI, bank transfer and cheque. Instalment plans are available for longer courses.

Can you help me choose a course?

Yes. Counselling is free. Call us or select the guidance option in the form.

Do you teach school students?

Yes. We run CBSE and GSEB batches for Std. 1 to 10.
