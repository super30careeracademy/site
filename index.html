Questions about the course

What will I learn in the AI MasterClass?

Five core topics: YouTube channel creation, motion graphics for brands, thumbnail creation, website building with AI and detailed prompt writing.

Who is the course for?

Students, young adults, creators and local business owners who want practical AI skills.

Do I need to know programming?

The five core topics focus on using AI tools. Programming is not one of them.

Is the class online or at the campus?

Classes are held in person at our Dindoli campus in Surat.

What are the fees and batch dates?

Message us on WhatsApp at 96645 68362 and we will share the current fees, dates and timings.

Can I attend a demo first?

Yes. Ask us to book a free demo class before you enrol.

Will I earn money after the course?

We cannot promise income. The course helps you build work you can show to employers or clients, and results depend on your practice and effort.
